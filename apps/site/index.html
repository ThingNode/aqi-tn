The network

Forty-five stations,
where children actually are.

Fifteen on traffic corridors today, expanding to thirty corridor units and fifteen inside school grounds — each validated on rotation against reference-grade equipment.

  • PM2.5, CO2 and NOx/VOC index
  • Five-minute resolution, published calibration record
  • Public feeds ingested and attributed, never blended

One sentence,
each morning.

Register your hours and your nearest station. Each morning you get the day's advisory in Sinhala, Tamil and English — with the number it came from, so you can forward it and defend it.

The question underneath

Does the corridor outside change the child inside?

Exposure at the school gate, cross-referenced against aggregate paediatric respiratory admissions — the first study of its kind in South Asia. Ward-level and anonymised: no record about any child, ever.
